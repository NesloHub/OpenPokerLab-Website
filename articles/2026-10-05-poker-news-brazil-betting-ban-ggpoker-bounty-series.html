Poker's Week: Brazil's Betting Crackdown, GGPoker's Bounty Series, and More

Published October 05, 2026 · OpenPokerLab Editorial

Brazil's Online Betting Ban Looms

Brazil is moving closer to a ban on online betting, according to a weekly gambling news roundup from PokerStrategy. While the exact scope and timing of the proposed restrictions remain unclear, the development signals growing regulatory pressure on the online gambling sector in Latin America's largest market. For poker players and operators, a ban could mean reduced access to real-money online poker and a shift in the competitive landscape. Brazil has long been a significant source of traffic for international poker sites, and any crackdown would likely ripple through the industry.

GGPoker's $80M Bounty Series Kicks Off with Ocean KO

With the WSOP Online now concluded, GGPoker is wasting no time in launching its next major tournament festival: the $80 million Bounty Series. The series is headlined by the Ocean KO, a bounty format that adds a progressive knockout element to the action. Bounty tournaments have become increasingly popular because they encourage aggressive play and give players more ways to cash. GGPoker's decision to put a bounty event at the forefront of an $80M series underscores how central these formats have become to online poker schedules.

WPT Global's 2-for-1 Promo

WPT Global is also keeping the momentum going after its Overdrive Series. The site has launched a 2-for-1 promotion that gives players two shots for $5.50, effectively doubling their tournament entries at a low buy-in. Promotions like this are designed to attract recreational players and boost field sizes, which in turn creates bigger prize pools and more value for regulars. It's a classic acquisition play, but one that can be effective in a crowded online market.

CoinPoker's CoinMasters and ggggUnit's Historic Run

CoinPoker's CoinMasters series has produced a notable storyline: player ggggUnit made history by securing the final seat through a series of seven coins. With the Global Online Poker Championship now finished and The One approaching, CoinPoker is positioning its series as a pathway to high-stakes opportunities. The "seven coins, one final seat" narrative adds a layer of drama to the satellite-style format, which rewards persistence and strategic play across multiple stages.

Calvin Anderson Wins Third Bracelet of 2026

Calvin Anderson has won his third bracelet of 2026 and the eighth of his career, according to PokerStrategy. The victory came in an online WSOP series event, further cementing Anderson's reputation as one of the most consistent tournament players in the game. Winning multiple bracelets in a single year is a rare feat, and doing so in online events highlights the growing prestige of online bracelet races. Anderson's success also reflects the broader trend of online series offering bracelet events that carry the same weight as their live counterparts.

Strategy Training: Red Line Mastery

For players looking to improve their non-showdown winnings, Run It Once has launched Red Line Mastery, described as the most extensive course ever on the topic. The course is currently available at a 20% discount. The "red line" refers to winnings from hands that don't go to showdown, and mastering it can help players win more pots without needing strong holdings. It's a nuanced area of poker strategy that many players overlook, making this course a potentially valuable resource for those looking to add folds and aggression to their game.

JackPoker's Bomb Pot Action Continues

JackPoker is extending its Lucky 8S Bombpot Fest through October 11. The promotion features daily challenges and Instant Cash opportunities, keeping bomb pot action going for another stretch. Bomb pots, where every player is dealt a hand and forced to put money in before the flop, create high-variance, action-packed situations that appeal to players looking for a different pace. By extending the festival, JackPoker is catering to a niche but enthusiastic segment of the player base.

What It All Means

This week's news highlights a few key themes in the poker world:

  • Regulatory uncertainty: Brazil's potential ban is a reminder that online poker operates in a constantly shifting legal environment. Players and operators should keep an eye on how this develops.
  • Bounty and promotional formats dominate: GGPoker's $80M Bounty Series and WPT Global's 2-for-1 promo show that sites are competing for attention with innovative formats and value-added offers.
  • Online bracelets matter: Calvin Anderson's third bracelet of 2026 in an online series reinforces that online poker's biggest stages are now on par with live events.
  • Strategy education evolves: Courses like Red Line Mastery indicate that players are hungry for advanced, specialized training.

As the poker calendar moves forward, these stories will continue to shape the ecosystem. Whether it's regulatory changes, tournament innovations, or player achievements, the game remains dynamic and full of storylines worth following.
