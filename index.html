PokerEquity Lab

See how often each hand wins. Draw the ranges on a 13x13 matrix, add a board and press calculate.

  • Equity for 2 to 10 players - exact counting or Monte Carlo
  • Range weights of 100 / 75 / 50 / 25 % with a range language
  • Board builder and the made hand written out in words

Poker Notes

Write a read on any opponent while you play, tag it in your own colours and find it again in one keystroke. Optional OCR hover boxes show your notes on the table.
