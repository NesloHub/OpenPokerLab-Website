Deciphering HUD Stats & Exploits

Understand what the numbers mean and how to adjust your game against each player archetype.

VPIP

Voluntarily Put In Pot

Percentage of hands where the player willingly puts money into the pot preflop (calls, limps, raises).

< 18%: Very Tight / Nit
20 - 28%: Solid Regular
> 35%: Loose Fish / Target
PFR

Preflop Raise Frequency

How often the player enters the pot with an open raise or re-raise rather than limping or calling.

VPIP/PFR Gap > 15%: Passive calling station
Gap < 5%: Aggressive tight reg
3-Bet%

Preflop 3-Bet Frequency

Percentage of opportunities where a player re-raises an open raiser preflop.

< 5%: Only QQ+, AK
7 - 10%: Optimal / Balanced
> 14%: 4-Bet them relentlessly
Fold to 3B%

Fold to 3-Bet Rate

How often a player surrenders when facing a re-raise after opening the pot.

> 70%: Auto-profit 3-bet bluff
< 40%: Only 3-bet for value

Yes! Because PokerVision HUD uses advanced computer vision to recognize tables and cards visually, it is compatible with virtually all modern poker sites that display screen names and bet action badges.

No! The v1.0 release is published as a self-contained portable bundle. All .NET runtime files, OpenCV native libraries, and Tesseract optical OCR files are bundled directly in the folder. Just unzip and run.
What happens if players leave or join the table? ▼
PokerVision HUD dynamically updates seat lineups whenever you middle-click on the table or when Autopilot triggers. If a new player sits down, their name is scanned and their stats immediately load from your database.

Yes! Your entire player library is saved in a standard players.json file. You can send this file to a friend, and they can load it via "📂 LOAD DB" or replace their local file.
