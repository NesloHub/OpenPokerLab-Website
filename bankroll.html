BANKROLL MANAGEMENT

Protect your edge. Survive the variance.

THEORY

The Golden Rules of BRM

Bankroll Management (BRM) is the mathematical shield that prevents you from going broke during inevitable downswings. Even the best players in the world experience variance.

  • Cash Games (NLHE): Minimum 40-50 Buy-ins for your limit. If you play aggressively, aim for 100 Buy-ins.
  • Tournaments (MTTs): High variance requires a minimum of 100-200 average Buy-ins.
  • Spin & Go / Jackpots: Extreme variance. 200+ Buy-ins is mandatory.
  • Shot Taking: Only take a shot at a higher limit if you have 5-10 extra buy-ins to lose, and strictly drop down if you lose them.
