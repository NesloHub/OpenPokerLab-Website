PREFLOP RANGES

Standard 6-Max Raise First In (RFI) guidelines based on GTO.

1. UTG / Lojack (LJ)
Opens ~18.5%
Pocket Pairs: 77+
Suited Ax: A2s+
Suited Broadways & Kings: K9s+, Q9s+, JTs, T9s
Suited Connectors: 98s
Offsuit Hands: ATo+, KJo+

*You play the tightest range here because 5 players act behind you.

2. Hijack (HJ)
Opens ~23.0%
Pocket Pairs: 55+
Suited Ax: A2s+
Suited Broadways & Kings: K5s+, Q9s+, J9s+, T8s+
Suited Connectors: 98s, 87s, 76s, 65s, 54s
Offsuit Hands: A9o+, KTo+, QTo+, JTo
3. Cutoff (CO)
Opens ~30.0%
Pocket Pairs: 22+
Suited Ax: A2s+
Suited Broadways & Kings: K3s+, Q8s+, J8s+, T8s+
Suited Connectors & Gappers: 97s+, 87s, 76s, 65s, 54s
Offsuit Hands: A8o+, K9o+, QTo+, JTo
4. Button (BTN)
Opens ~45-48%
Pocket Pairs: 22+
Suited Ax: A2s+
Suited Broadways & Kings: K2s+, Q2s+, J4s+, T5s+
Suited Connectors & Gappers: 96s+, 86s+, 75s+, 64s+, 54s
Offsuit Hands: A2o+, K5o+, Q9o+, J9o+, T8o+, 98o

*The best position in poker. Attack the blinds relentlessly with a very wide range.

5. Small Blind (SB) vs Big Blind
Opens ~40.0%
Pocket Pairs: 22+
Suited Ax: A2s+
Suited Broadways & Kings: K2s+, Q5s+, J7s+, T7s+
Suited Connectors & Gappers: 97s+, 86s+, 76s, 65s, 54s
Offsuit Hands: A2o+, K9o+, Q9o+, J9o+, T9o

*Note: When SB opens, the standard sizing should be larger (3x or 3.5x) due to being out of position postflop.
