PREFLOP RANGES

Standard 6-Max Raise First In (RFI) guidelines based on GTO.

Under the Gun (UTG)
Opens ~15%
Pairs: 77+
Suited: ATs+, KTs+, QJs, JTs
Offsuit: AQo+

*You play the tightest range here because 5 players act behind you.

Hijack (HJ)
Opens ~20%
Pairs: 55+
Suited: A8s+, K9s+, QTs+, J9s+, T9s
Offsuit: AJo+, KQo
Cutoff (CO)
Opens ~28%
Pairs: 22+
Suited: A2s+, K7s+, Q9s+, J8s+, T8s+, 98s, 87s
Offsuit: ATo+, KTo+, QTo+, JTo
The Button (BTN)
Opens ~45%
Pairs: 22+
Suited: A2s+, K2s+, Q2s+, J6s+, T6s+, 96s+, 85s+, 75s+, 65s, 54s
Offsuit: A2o+, K8o+, Q8o+, J8o+, T8o+

*The best position in poker. Attack the blinds relentlessly with a very wide range.

Small Blind (SB) vs Big Blind
Opens ~40%
Pairs: 22+
Suited: A2s+, K2s+, Q5s+, J7s+, T7s+, 97s+, 86s+, 76s, 65s, 54s
Offsuit: A2o+, K9o+, Q9o+, J9o+, T9o

*Note: When SB opens, the standard sizing should be larger (3x or 3.5x) due to being out of position postflop.
