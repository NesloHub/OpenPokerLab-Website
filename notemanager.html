Do you play on Unibet or other poker sites that block traditional HUDs? Do you lose track of the "fish" and "regs" when multi-tabling? Here is the solution.

Poker Note Manager is a lightning-fast, web-based app designed to give you a "Heads-Up Display" without breaking the poker sites' Terms of Service. It runs 100% securely in your own browser and lets you create visual notes on your opponents in under 2 seconds.

🌟 Why use Poker Note Manager?

  • 100% Legal & Secure: The app does not interact with your poker client. No bots, no screen-scraping, and absolutely zero risk of getting banned.
  • 📸 Screenshot Magic (Ctrl+V): Forget about typing complicated usernames in the middle of an intense hand. Take a screenshot of your opponent, press Ctrl+V, and the app automatically builds a note card containing the image!
  • 🎨 Fully Customizable Tags: Don't want to be locked into default categories? Build your own! Create tags like "Whale", "GTO", or "Donk", pick any color from the color palette, and the app automatically adjusts the text color for perfect readability.
  • ⚡ Built for Multi-tabling: "Live search" makes it easy to filter specific cards, and the "Clear Board" feature lets you wipe your screen clean with one click when your session is over.
  • 💾 Auto-Save & Smart Backups: Everything is saved automatically the very millisecond you type it. Exporting a backup now securely saves both your player database and your custom tags in one single file.

📖 How to use the app (Step-by-step)

The app is designed to be the fastest tool you have ever used at the poker tables. Here is your new workflow:

1. Create a player mid-hand (The Ninja Method)

When an opponent does something noteworthy and you need to act fast:

  1. Use your operating system's screenshot shortcut (On Windows: Win + Shift + S / On Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4).
  2. Drag a box over the player's avatar and name in your poker client.
  3. Click over to the Poker Note Manager and press Ctrl + V (Paste) anywhere on the page.
  4. BAM! The app instantly creates a new card with your screenshot. Click a color tag, finish playing your hand, and write a note when you have the time. Everything is auto-saved.

2. Name your opponents (When things calm down)

When the hand is over, or when you are reviewing your session:

  1. Click the temporary name (e.g., Player_12345) right below the image.
  2. Type the player's real username from the poker site and press 'Enter'.
  3. The image, tags, and your notes are now permanently bound to this name in your database.

3. Personalize your setup (Manage Tags)

  1. Click the blue "⚙️ Manage Tags" button at the top of the screen.
  2. Type a name for your new tag, select a color from the color picker, and hit Add.
  3. You can also delete default tags you don't use by clicking the trash can icon.

4. Keep track of your tables

  • Search the board: Playing 8 tables and have 40 cards open? Use the "Search active cards..." bar in the middle of the screen. Type two letters of the player's name, and all other cards are instantly hidden so you can focus.
  • Clear Board: When your session is done, simply press "Clear Board". It removes all cards from your screen so you are ready for your next grind.

5. IMPORTANT: Remember to take Backups! 💾

The app saves everything locally in your browser's memory (LocalStorage). If you clear your browsing history or cookies, your data will disappear!

  • Click "💾 Export Backup" at least once a week. This downloads a small, secure file to your computer containing all your players and custom tags.
  • If disaster strikes, simply click "📂 Import Backup", select your latest file, and your entire database is restored in a split second.

💡 Pro-Tip for Windows users:
Use Alt + Tab to seamlessly switch between the poker client and the Note Manager. Take a screenshot -> Alt + Tab -> Ctrl + V. The entire process takes less than 2 seconds!
