OPEN-SOURCE EQUITY CALCULATOR

PokerEquity Lab

PokerStove Replacement — Equity & Ranges, Fully Offline

A free Texas Hold'em equity and range calculator for Windows. Run 2 to 10 players with exact enumeration or up to 5,000,000 Monte Carlo runouts, build ranges by painting the 13x13 matrix (JJ+:50 weights included), pick from 15 one-click presets, and add a flop, turn and river with a plain English hand readout for every player. Self-contained: no installer, no .NET runtime, no account and no network calls.
